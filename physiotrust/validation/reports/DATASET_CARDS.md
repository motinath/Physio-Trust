# PhysioTrust Dataset Cards

## Dataset 1: MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database
- **Source**: PhysioNet / Beth Israel Hospital.
- **Modality**: 2-channel Ambulatory ECG.
- **Sampling Frequency**: 360 Hz.
- **Volume**: 48 half-hour 2-channel ECG recordings.

---

## Dataset 2: PPG-DaLiA Wrist PPG & Motion Database
- **Source**: UCI Machine Learning Repository / University of Freiburg.
- **Modality**: Wrist PPG, 3-axis Accelerometer, Skin Temp, EDA.
- **Sampling Frequency**: 64 Hz (PPG), 32 Hz (ACC).
- **Volume**: 15 subjects during daily life routines.
